# Definitions: Payment Domain

Ubiquitous Language for the Payment lifecycle.

## Core Terms

- **Payment**: An event or record representing the transfer of funds to reduce a specific financial obligation.
- **Payor**: The entity or person making the payment.
- **Payee**: The entity or person receiving the payment.
- **Transaction Date**: The actual date the funds were transferred.
- **Payment Reference**: An identifier linking the payment to an external system (e.g., bank transfer ID).
- **Settlement**: The result of a payment that satisfies a debt obligation.
